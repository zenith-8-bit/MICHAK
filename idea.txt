CONTROLS:
Controls

Mouse steering: gone (M toggle too).
S + Space, Immelmann: half loop up, half roll out. You end up reversed and about 65 m higher.
W + Space, Split-S: half roll, then half loop down. You end up reversed, about 70 m lower and faster. It refuses with “TOO LOW FOR SPLIT-S” if there isn’t enough air under you.
Tab, Cobra: the nose swings past vertical while you keep flying straight. Speed drops to about 34, then it snaps back.
Cooldowns and old moves: the combos have a 2.5 s cooldown. Plain Space is still the barrel roll, and double-tap A/D still snap-turns.
On screen: the combo name flashes on screen, and the key hints show top-right.

Boost and brake animation

Shift: the sonic boom is unchanged. It now adds an afterburner plume and light, a nose-dip punch, speed stretch, and engine shudder.
Ctrl:
Airbrake panels swing out and the nose flares up.
Shock rings collapse inward on the nose.
Wingtip vapor appears, also during hard pulls and the cobra.

amera and feel

Inertial delay: inputs pass through a short delay and an actuator lag, and the gust torque arrives through the same lag. In calm air a bank takes about 0.23 s to reach 0.4 rad; in heavy wind about 0.77 s. Gusts have to be anticipated.
Boost: the camera pulls back from about 29 m to 54 m, FOV widens to 82, the far world softens, and a radial blur pulls at the screen edges.
Ctrl or glide: focus does a quick rack-focus hunt, then locks. A sharpening pulse makes nearby rock and cloud textures pop, and it hits harder close to terrain.
Edge tint: red for danger, green for a manta within about 300 m. It fades in, holds a couple of seconds, and fades out even if the cause persists. Peak strength is about 60%. If you want it subtler or bolder, change the two multipliers near the end of _push_screen_fx in camera_director.gd.
Context-Reticle Morphing: When looking toward a checkpoint or a poacher radio-interference target, have the central crosshair subtly morph from a dot into a segmented targeting bracket, confirming that the player's nose is correctly aligned to execute the protocol task (like releasing fake signals).

Minimalist State Transitions: Ensure that edge widgets (like the weather compass and hull status) completely dissolve into near-invisible transparency during calm cruising, fading back in smoothly only when wind speeds spike or environmental hazards are detected.
Acoustic Song Layering: When tracking Mantas, instead of abruptly changing the music track, cross-fade a warm, breathing acoustic layer or organic harmonic drone into the existing ambient mix the moment you enter their proximity threshold.

War Zone & Mountain Audio Dampening: Flying near automated ground poacher zones should introduce a low-frequency mechanical hum and distant static interference over the radio comms, contrasting sharply with the echoing, wind-swept reverb experienced when skimming close to mountain faces.

Random Wind-Front Visual Cues: Introduce faint, semi-transparent wind-streak particles and sudden audio pressure drops when the plane randomly encounters face-to-speed wind currents, giving players a visual warning split-seconds before the airframe gets pushed off-course.
